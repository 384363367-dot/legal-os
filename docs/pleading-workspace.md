# Pleading and Evidence Document Workspace

This module provides a source-locked interface for producing Chinese legal documents after litigation analysis, evidence mapping and legal research.

Supported: civil complaint/defence, commercial arbitration application/defence, labour/personnel arbitration application/defence and evidence catalogue.

Every populated document must trace to user-confirmed parties/dates/amounts/procedural posture/relief, evidence supporting each material fact, and current source-verified legal authority. If a material value is missing, contradictory or inferred, stop or mark for review.

Every complaint, application or answer is paired with an independent evidence catalogue. Incomplete evidence produces a `待补证 / 内部草稿` catalogue rather than omission. Labour/personnel arbitration uses dedicated variants and does not request labour-arbitration costs from the opposing party.

Before final use, check fact–evidence–authority–relief/defence alignment, identity/jurisdiction/amount/date/case number/procedure, numbering/layout, cross-artifact alignment, removal of internal strategy, and separate authorization for drafting/review/sending/filing/signing.
