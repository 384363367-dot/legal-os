# Formal Correspondence Workspace

Source-locked workflow for lawyer letters, payment/performance notices, replies and situation statements. It is a drafting/template/quality-gate layer, not a sending or filing service.

Before an external clean version, verify purpose/audience/sender/recipient, facts/amounts/dates/obligations/evidence, deadlines/delivery/attachments, current legal authority where cited, removal of internal strategy, and separate authorization for sending/service/filing/signing.
