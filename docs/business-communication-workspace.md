# Legal OS Business Communication Workspace

Generic source-locked workflow for internal messages, business WeChat/email, phone/meeting talking points and ordinary working communication that is not a formal legal notice.

Identify audience/channel/objective/role/verified facts/output/sending permission; draft purpose first, then verified facts, requested action, owner/deadline and reply method. Scan for admissions, concessions, new prices, settlement, waiver, release, termination or external commitments. Route formal notices to T-06, legal assertions to T-05, data conflicts to T-08, and formal reports to T-10.
