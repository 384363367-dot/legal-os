# Correspondence quality gate

Read the public [external-expression boundary](../../legal-os-unified-intake/references/external-expression-boundary.md) by audience before release. It supplements, rather than duplicates, source, legal, template and authorization checks.

Pass only when sender, recipient, matter, facts, amount, dates, deadline, delivery channel, attachments, and requested response are verified against supplied material. If law is cited, verify current effectiveness and the exact proposition.

For a counterparty or opponent version, check that the text does not proactively introduce an unraised defence, exception, nonessential legal route, evidence reserve, negotiation limit or fallback argument. A minimum response is allowed only where the issue was raised, is necessary to complete the current claim/response, is required by law/procedure/institution, or is needed to keep the statement truthful and non-contradictory. Remove internal strategy, process notes and contingency analysis.

For a court, arbitral, administrative or regulatory submission, confirm that any required disclosure or response is present and truthful. For internal, client or management analysis, confirm that material risks, adverse facts and relevant opposing views have not been compressed into a favourable-only summary.

For a payment or collection letter, verify the fact chain `legal relationship → our performance → payment obligation formed → due → unpaid`; do not add legal exposition or special modules unless the actual issue triggers them. Hold when the text could admit liability, waive rights, create a settlement or termination effect, omit a mandatory disclosure, or be sent without separate authorization.
