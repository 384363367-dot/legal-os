# Public external-expression boundary

This reference controls what belongs in an external expression after facts, evidence and authority have been verified. It is a disclosure-selection rule, not a substitute for source verification, legal duties, internal analysis or authorization.

## Apply by audience

- **Counterparty or opponent:** state the minimum necessary issue, requested action, truthful factual foundation and required response. Do not volunteer an unraised defence, nonessential legal route, evidence reserve or gap, negotiation floor, fallback argument or complete contingency plan.
- **Court, arbitral institution, government or regulator:** include every disclosure, response, fact, legal basis and procedural item required by law, procedure or the receiving institution. The boundary cannot be used to omit a necessary logical link or to present an unresolved fact as established.
- **Internal, client or management analysis:** keep material risks, adverse facts, opposing views, evidence gaps, alternatives and response options complete. Do not compress analysis into a favourable-only account.

## Selection checks

Before an external version is labelled ready, confirm:

1. the current procedural or substantive objective and requested relief/response are confirmed;
2. each material assertion is supported, identified as a party statement, or expressly marked pending outside the external text;
3. the selected content is necessary for the audience and current issue;
4. mandatory legal, contractual and procedural disclosures remain present and truthful;
5. admissions, waivers, concessions, settlement effects, termination effects and new commitments have a separate authorization decision; and
6. internal labels such as pending, unverified, evidence gap and drafting note do not leak into the external artifact.

For external, submission, approval, signature, archive or other formal delivery, inspect the whole finished artifact: body, title, subtitle, headers, footers, filename, version label, attachment names, contents page and cover. A finished Word artifact must contain no historical version labels (“草案”, “送审稿”, “修订稿”, “简版”, “内部版”, “优化版”, “压缩版”), internal communication or opinions, descriptions of modifications or deletions, or generation/process labels (“给我先看”, “AI生成”, “临时”, “草稿供讨论”) in any of these locations. Remove such process content throughout the finished Word artifact; preserve substantive facts and legally necessary procedural dates in the body.

Minimum necessary disclosure does not mean minimum necessary reasoning. Preserve the complete internal analysis in the appropriate internal artifact and keep the external selection traceable to its source and function.
