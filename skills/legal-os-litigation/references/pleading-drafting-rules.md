# Pleading drafting rules

Apply these rules to Chinese civil litigation (first instance, appeal, retrial and enforcement), criminal defence, criminal complaints and victim representation, and the already supported arbitration work. Adapt analysis to the actual role and stage. Formal file generation still requires an approved procedure-specific template.

The public external-expression boundary in `legal-os-unified-intake/references/external-expression-boundary.md` supplies the audience rule. R13–R16 apply it to pleadings without replacing procedural disclosure duties or restricting complete internal/client analysis.

## R1 — Resolve the procedure variant first

Classify `procedure_type`, `pleading_role` and `document_variant` before template resolution. Civil litigation, commercial arbitration, labour/personnel arbitration and other procedures do not share identical requests, cost rules or mandatory fields. Stop with `PROCEDURE_VARIANT_REQUIRED` when the variant cannot be established from current material.

At new intake or receipt of a new procedural document, scan deadlines before merits work. Record actual service evidence, the verified rule or order, starting event, computation method, expiry date, irreversible consequence and backward work plan with owners. Cover applicable defence, evidence, hearing, appeal and application periods separately. A document date cannot substitute for actual service. Mark unknown service or law as pending and record its impact. Existing matters reuse the file and check new procedural changes.

For criminal matters, identify defence, complainant or victim role and investigation, prosecution, trial or review stage. Analyse charged conduct, offence elements, evidence legality, applicable proof standard and procedural rights using verified Chinese authority. Do not transplant civil burden allocation or foreign discovery, jury or certification rules. Civil appeal, retrial and enforcement analysis must likewise address the actual decision, challenge grounds, enforceable instrument and stage-specific scope.

## R2 — Analyse first, confirm the material route, then draft

Subject to the explicit user-directed draft-first exception below, apply these stages to substantive drafting for the litigation and supported arbitration matters in R1, including work directly serving an identified case. General non-litigation lawyer letters, payment notices and claim letters remain with their existing correspondence workflow. The conclusions-table and section-confirmation requirements introduced here do not extend to those general letters merely because they concern rights. Ordinary explanations and isolated non-substantive wording/formatting edits do not require renewed substantive approvals. For a case-related letter, record the identified case and procedural purpose that makes this rule applicable.

**Stage 1 — substantive analysis and recommendation in normal dialogue.** Read the actual supplied materials and latest verifiable matter status; identify the represented party, parties' relationship, procedural position and current objective. Within that scope, actively identify additional materially supported claims, defences, demands, procedural routes or compatible alternatives that the user did not name; explain their source support and risk, and do not elevate speculative or unsupported possibilities. For every requested or proposed claim, defence, demand or response, give a readable account of the relief or response and amount; relevant contract terms and current verified legal basis; elements and establishment/due conditions; supporting or adverse evidence by name, source and proof function; the source and formula for amounts and dates; material unverified facts or gaps and their effect; and the recommended route and reasons. For an answer, address the opposing party's actual requests, alleged facts and legal grounds item by item, as R4 requires. Keep confirmed facts, party statements, evidence, legal evaluation, inference and uncertainty distinct.

Do not let a small or non-dispositive gap stop analysis of unrelated issues. Mark the affected issue and its consequence, then continue as far as the available sources permit. Without reliable evidence of when an obligation became due, do not invent a due date, overdue start date or interest period; analyze other supported issues separately. Stage 1 is the substantive analysis in dialogue. Unless the user explicitly requests drafting first under the exception below, do not yet create the formal pleading or a Word evidence catalogue.

Under the normal sequence, after presenting the analysis and recommendation, obtain the user's clear choice in the conversation on the material claims, defences, demands, amounts or rights position to carry forward. A broad initial request such as “I want to sue” or “prepare a complaint” states the task; by itself it does not confirm a route or relief later recommended from the materials. The user is choosing the business/legal position to instruct, not certifying the analysis: professional source and law verification remains the workflow's responsibility. Once the user clearly confirms the recommended route or selects another supported route, proceed directly to Stage 2 without asking again for permission to draft. Confirmation does not replace any required formal sign-off or authorize sending, service, filing, signing, publication or other external action; those actions retain their separate authorization boundary.

**Stage 2 — formal drafting after confirmation.** Before formal drafting, complete the matter's single conclusions table in the existing internal ledger. Each issue records the represented role and objective, our request/defence/accusation or rights-protection position, the opposing claim or charge where one exists (otherwise mark not applicable), amount or other core matter, factual and legal conclusion, evidence support and key gaps, true internal assessment, proposed or confirmed external position, confirmation status and its source. For a civil claimant analyse our requested remedies and elements. For a civil defendant analyse actual opposing claims and our defences. For criminal defence analyse charged conduct, offence elements, evidence legality, proof standard and procedural rights. For a criminal complainant or victim analyse the alleged harm/conduct, supporting and adverse evidence, requested lawful procedural action and rights-protection route. Do not invent an opponent claim to fill a column.

Completion requires substantive issue analysis based on the available materials. Each material row must connect a reasoned provisional or firm conclusion to facts, evidence support, the applicable legal analysis and any unverified authority, gaps and their effect, and the proposed position or decision needed. An empty table, headings alone, or unanalyzed pending labels do not satisfy this prerequisite. Unresolved matters may remain only with their analysed impact and action/hold recorded. If no supported conclusion can be drawn, keep the affected assertion on hold. A table's existence alone never opens drafting. It is the common conclusion source for pleadings, hearing positions and settlement options. Urgency, an old draft or model judgment never waives this prerequisite. The sole exception is the user's explicit request to draft first. Record that instruction and scope, preserve source/law verification marks in the internal working draft, and complete the table as work continues. This explicit instruction permits provisional drafting while Stage 1 or route confirmation is still pending. It does not deem an unconfirmed position confirmed and does not waive section confirmation or final quality controls.

Before formal section drafting, submit the whole-document structure card and obtain the user's confirmation. The card records the document function, section order, conclusion-led headings, argument/evidence anchors and necessary decisions, linked to the conclusions table. Route confirmation alone does not confirm the structure. Then draft section by section. The explicit draft-first exception waives only the conclusions-table/normal analysis prerequisite for its stated scope, while structure and section confirmation remain required. Submit each section separately for user confirmation, including its conclusion, argument, evidence support and necessary decisions. Assemble the final full text only after every section is confirmed. A route confirmation or general drafting authorization does not confirm unwritten sections. Reuse already confirmed sections without repeated approval. If facts or conclusions change, explain the impact and reconfirm only affected sections. Track confirmation source, date and version in the ledger.

Draft the confirmed, supported scope through the existing procedure and template controls. If an appropriate approved variant is unavailable, hold formal file generation under `TEMPLATE_REQUIRED`; that does not erase the Stage 1 analysis. Existing R1–R16, external-expression limits, template requirements, release labels and quality gates continue to apply and are not replaced by the strategy confirmation or a light delivery check.

For an existing matter, reuse the confirmed strategy and substantive verification for edits that do not materially change a claim, defence, amount, deadline, right or position. A new claim, waiver of a material right, material amount change or material defence change requires renewed analysis and confirmation only for the affected issue; preserve unaffected verified work. Complete substantive verification during Stage 1 and reuse it while the source, version and legal/time applicability remain unchanged. After Stage 2, apply the existing pleading quality gate with an independent review of substantive conclusions, computations, cross-document consistency and file usability. Reuse unaffected verification evidence. Reopen only affected verification when new or contradictory material, a source/version or temporal-applicability change, or a material calculation requires it.

The external pleading should use the strongest compatible route that covers the confirmed outcome without unnecessary admissions or exposure. Add an alternative position only when the primary route is incomplete and the alternative is factually and legally compatible.

## R3 — Applications must establish each requested remedy

For every material claim or defence, connect internally:

`confirmed objective → executable relief or defence and calculation → legal/procedural elements → material facts → evidence → jurisdiction, limitation, required procedure and verified current authority → legal conclusion`

Adapt the chain to the document's function; do not mechanically expose the entire internal chain in an external pleading. Do not replace facts with abstract legal conclusions or omit an element merely to keep the document short. High information density does not justify omitting necessary logical links, procedural information, amount basis or issue explanation.

## R4 — Answers use an issue-scope gate

Respond to the opposing party's actual claims, alleged facts and legal grounds. Do not volunteer a new claim basis, admit an unproved fact, open an unnecessary adverse exception, or disclose internal fallback strategy. Use five questions for each actual request or accusation internally. What is asserted? What evidence supports it? Do its elements and burden of proof establish it? How can we rebut it? What further evidence, checks or strategy are needed? Keep unraised issues out of external responsive documents in principle, while retaining necessary affirmative defences, procedural objections and legally required positions. Potential major risks belong in internal analysis and do not automatically expand the external scope. Criminal work follows R1's role-specific path.

Screen possible defences in this order only as applicable: dispositive procedure; prior agreement, satisfaction or extinguishment; missing elements; amount/calculation; compatible alternative defence.

## R5 — Use conclusion-led headings

Each substantive heading should state a conclusion a decision-maker could adopt. Use the unit:

`conclusion → material facts → evidence anchor → necessary legal evaluation → effect on the claim or defence`

Avoid empty headings such as “relevant circumstances” and repeated conclusory adjectives.

## R6 — Cite authority by function

Before placing authority in the external pleading, ask whether it decides the issue, is needed to complete the claim or defence, responds to an argument actually raised, and whether its proviso or exception would unnecessarily enlarge an adverse issue. Verify current status and an official or otherwise authoritative source. Keep nonessential or contingency authority in the internal authority package.

## R7 — Do not exceed the evidence

Prefer complete originals and signed records to screenshots, OCR or recollection. Keep ambiguous handwriting, amounts, dates or identities unverified. A payment record proves only the parties, time, amount and displayed purpose shown on it; do not infer an unsupported allocation or full settlement. Record the source of each material fact. In internal work, distinguish evidence-supported facts, party statements, opponent assertions, facts still to be proved, disputed/unconfirmed facts, legal evaluations and inferences. An external pleading may state a fact to be proved when necessary, but must not describe it as established beyond source support.

## R8 — Determine delivery scope at Stage 2

Maintain the internal evidence index regardless of requested deliverables. Generate Word, PDF, Excel, a separate evidence catalogue or other files only within the user's explicit delivery request. If delivery scope is unclear, ask at delivery. Where the selected procedure requires a catalogue, identify that requirement and clarify the requested submission package before final delivery. A requested catalogue remains independent and synchronized with the pleading and evidence materials. Incomplete evidence keeps it draft-only with gaps recorded. Formal files require the approved variant under R12. This replaces automatic paired-file generation and does not remove internal mapping or procedural duties. For criminal and special procedures determine the actual applicable catalogue/material-list duty from the verified procedure, rather than extending civil catalogue fields by default.

For litigation deliveries, set `paired_evidence_catalog_required` from the actual requested or verified procedural requirement and record its source. Resolve each requested artifact through the template runtime. Its default selects one template. Use `resolve --with-evidence-catalog` only when the requested or procedurally required set includes a paired catalogue. Copy, populate and deliver only that set. Run `audit-docx` on each produced artifact and the existing release gate on the actual delivery set. A missing unrequested catalogue template does not block single-document resolution. Requested or legally required materials retain approval, path, hash and completeness checks. Detailed catalogues use `evidence-catalog-detailed`, while the image carrier page uses `evidence-material-page`. The generic litigation structure reference guides drafting and is not a substitute for an approved procedure-specific template.

## R9 — No independent evidence-source chapter

Do not create an independent “证据和证据来源” or equivalent chapter inside a complaint, application or answer. Use concise evidence anchors in the relevant factual paragraph when useful. Maintain evidence number, name, page location and proof purpose in the internal index and, when requested or procedurally required, the independent evidence catalogue.

## R10 — Keep the external catalogue lean and the internal map complete

The external catalogue normally uses `编号 | 证据名称 | 页码 | 证明事项`. A proof purpose must identify the claim, element, material fact or defence it supports. The private fact–evidence map additionally records source, formation date, original status, authenticity/completeness risk, corresponding proposition, gaps and review state. Maintain a timeline with key, relevant and background significance. Distinguish confirmed, unverified, missing and conflicting facts. Preserve conflicting accounts and their separate sources. Each outcome-material fact must resolve to evidence number, source page or other exact location and proof purpose internally. External paragraphs need not each display numbers and pages.

Review evidence for adverse facts, internal contradictions, source defects, potential admissions and opponent use. Record the impact, lawful response, gap owner, requested material/format and fallback if unavailable. Preserve original evidence. Never conceal, destroy or fabricate evidence to manage risk.

Do not cap the number of evidence items for an argument. Order relevant, authentic and probative evidence by weight, directness, relevance and importance, with core evidence first and corroboration following. Remove mechanical duplication only after preserving material proof. Insufficient direct evidence requires analysis of its effect, burden allocation, indirect evidence and lawful alternative defences, without treating an item count as a proof standard.

## R11 — Test alternative defences for compatibility

Place an alternative defence in the external answer only if the primary defence does not fully cover the claim, it does not negate a key primary fact or reasonably read as an admission, it has evidence and verified law, and disclosure does not materially expand the opposing party's attack path. Otherwise keep it in the internal hearing plan. Do not use internal/external separation to prohibit a legally necessary alternative defence, amount dispute or set-off. Explain its necessity and compatibility and obtain the affected position confirmation.

## R12 — The template controls the shell, not the adjudication path

The template controls visual identity, minimum functional sections and signature placement. It does not control paragraph count, create an unraised issue, supply a missing fact/evidence/authority, preserve a procedure-inapplicable cost request, or restore an evidence-source chapter. If the selected variant conflicts with the matter, stop with `TEMPLATE_VARIANT_REQUIRED` rather than silently converting it into a final document.

## R13 — Initial claimant pleadings use a single-party stance gate

For a complaint, arbitration application, payment-order application or equivalent first request filed for a claimant, applicant or creditor, build the external document around four questions only:

1. What legal relationship connects the parties?
2. What supported obligations has the represented party performed?
3. What obligation is due from the opposing party, and how has it been breached?
4. How do verified facts, evidence and authority establish each requested remedy?

Do not volunteer a hypothetical opponent defence, opponent evidence roadmap, opponent proof deficiency, proposed allocation of the opponent's burden, adjudicator investigation plan, nonessential adverse authority or the represented party's complete contingency response. Do not state an unverified favourable fact as established. Keep these items in the internal analysis, hearing plan, authority package or a separate procedural application as applicable.

Keep stage boundaries explicit: an initial or start-up pleading establishes the requested relief and necessary foundation; a responsive or argumentative pleading develops issues actually raised or formed in the proceeding. Do not import hypothetical response analysis into an initial pleading merely because an adverse fact could matter.

## R14 — Exceptions require a recorded function

An initial claimant pleading may address an adverse proposition or use a compatible alternative position only when at least one of the following is true:

- the opposing party has actually raised it in supplied correspondence, a prior pleading, an objection or another identified source;
- it is necessary to establish an element, jurisdiction, limitation, admissibility, a condition precedent or another required procedure;
- the primary route cannot fully support the requested outcome and the alternative is factually, evidentially and legally compatible;
- the selected court, tribunal, institution or document variant expressly requires the information.

Record the source, purpose and compatibility review in the internal workspace. Do not use an exception to introduce an unsupported concession, broaden an adverse issue or reveal internal fallback strategy.

## R15 — Separate content by procedural function

- **Initial complaint or application:** requested relief, supported performance, opposing breach, necessary facts, evidence anchors and verified authority serving the claim.
- **Internal matter analysis:** possible defences, risk, burden analysis, evidence gaps, contrary authority and response options.
- **Hearing plan:** questions, contradictions and follow-up paths.
- **Evidence comments:** responses to evidence actually submitted, including authenticity, legality, relevance and weight as applicable.
- **Closing or agency submission:** developed issues and arguments actually raised in the proceeding.
- **Investigation or evidence-production request:** the specific procedural request and its verified basis, kept separate from the initial pleading unless the selected procedure requires otherwise.

When external text crosses these boundaries, remove it from the external artifact and preserve it in the correct internal artifact; do not destroy the underlying analysis.

## R16 — Conditional language triggers contextual review

Terms such as “如果”, “即使”, “可能”, “如经查明” and “若对方不能证明” trigger `REVIEW_REQUIRED` in an initial claimant pleading. They do not automatically fail the document. Retain them only when R14 is satisfied and the sentence remains supported, compatible and necessary; otherwise replace the sentence with a positive, evidence-supported statement or move it to the internal workspace.

### Case-use control for initial claimant pleadings

- Use a case only when its verified adjudicative proposition materially supports a requested remedy or resolves an issue that must be addressed.
- Do not introduce a nonessential adverse case, summarize the opponent's case theory or provide a roadmap for distinguishing authorities not yet in issue.
- Keep directly relevant contrary material and contingency research in the internal authority package so that external selectivity does not become internal confirmation bias.
- State the adjudicative proposition concisely. Avoid long factual narratives.
- Do not use a case externally unless its source, identifier, adjudicative level and verification status satisfy the authority-package controls.

### Third-party-payment clause scenario

When the represented claimant relies on a direct payment obligation and the contract also refers to payment by a third party, structure the initial pleading in this order, but only to the extent supported by the actual contract, performance record and verified authority:

1. contract formation and effectiveness;
2. the represented party's delivery, service or work;
3. acceptance, settlement or other supported payment basis;
4. the amount and due date of the direct payment obligation;
5. the legally supported character and effect of the third-party-payment wording;
6. why an identified third-party commercial arrangement does or does not affect the direct obligation;
7. the established default and resulting liability;
8. executable relief, calculations and procedural costs where permitted.

Do not speculate about whether the third party paid, whether the opposing party pursued collection, what upstream records should be produced or whether a condition should be deemed fulfilled. Address those matters later only if evidence or an actual defence makes them material. Do not hard-code the substantive effect of third-party-payment wording across matters.

## Pair consistency gate

- Every pleading evidence reference must resolve to the internal evidence index and, when a catalogue is delivered, its corresponding catalogue item.
- Every catalogue item must support at least one claim, element, material fact or defence.
- Renumbering, renaming or changing a proof purpose requires synchronized updates across both artifacts.
- Unknown page ranges remain `待编页`; never invent pagination.
- Distinguish evidence submitted by the represented party from material filed by another party.
- Keep related pleadings, evidence catalogues, written submissions and hearing statements substantively consistent in party identity, dates, amounts, legal relationship, performance and core facts. If new evidence, verification, relief or procedure requires a change, record the difference and legal effect internally rather than leaving an unexplained material conflict.
