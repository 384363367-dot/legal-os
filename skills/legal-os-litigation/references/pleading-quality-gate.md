# Pleading quality gate

Pass only when each material fact and requested relief/defence is supported by supplied material, legal propositions are tied to current verified authority, and the DOCX source has passed the required structure, content and format-property checks.

For a claimant's initial complaint, arbitration application, payment-order application or equivalent first request, also confirm that:

- the current procedural or substantive objective and requested relief or defence scope are confirmed; necessary facts, amount basis, procedural information and logical links are present, and minimum disclosure has not become a material omission;
- the document maintains one represented-party stance and closes the supported chain from performance to due obligation, breach and relief;
- it does not invent or volunteer a hypothetical opponent defence, evidence roadmap, proof deficiency, burden-allocation plan or adjudicator investigation plan;
- it does not present an unverified favourable fact as established or disclose internal risk, fallback or response strategy;
- related procedural documents in the same matter are substantively consistent in party identity, dates, amounts, legal relationship, performance and core facts; any change caused by new evidence, further verification, changed relief, procedure or correction has a recorded difference and legal effect;
- internal pending, unverified, evidence-gap or drafting-process labels do not enter the external artifact, while complete internal analysis is preserved rather than compressed into a favourable-only account;
- every adverse proposition, compatible alternative position or conditional formulation admitted to the external document satisfies R14 and has a recorded source and function;
- case use is necessary, source-verified and claim-serving, while directly relevant contrary material remains recorded in the internal authority package;
- investigation, evidence-production, hearing and evidence-comment functions have not been mixed into the initial pleading unless the selected procedure expressly requires them.

A conditional-language match triggers `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, not automatic deletion or automatic failure. Remove unsupported or unnecessary text from the external artifact and preserve useful analysis in the correct internal artifact.

When a separate evidence catalogue is requested or required by the applicable procedure, verify that its numbering, evidence names and proof purposes match the pleading and evidence materials; the pleading itself must not contain an independent “证据和证据来源” chapter. If evidence is incomplete, label the catalogue `待补证 / 内部草稿` and keep the release state blocked or draft-only. Fail or hold for review when identity, amount, date, forum, case number, procedural posture, core evidence, cross-artifact mapping, source text, tables, numbering or required format properties conflict. Labour/personnel arbitration pleadings fail if they request arbitration costs from the other party. Keep drafting, internal review, sending, filing, and signing as separate authorization states.


## Integrated litigation review

Use this existing gate for all applicable litigation work, with role/stage-specific checks. Record reviewer, method, actual source coverage, findings and unresolved items. Independent review must return to raw materials and recompute figures, using a separate review task or an explicitly separate opposing-party/adjudicator review pass. State any independence or coverage limits. Do not infer review from a checklist alone.

- Check R1 deadline/service basis, procedural stage, irreversible nodes and current pending decisions.
- Check the single conclusions table was completed before formal drafting, or record the user's explicit early-drafting instruction. Verify the role-appropriate table contains substantive analysis rather than blank rows or unanalyzed pending labels. Verify the whole structure card was submitted and confirmed before section drafting, and each section's separate confirmation and affected-section reconfirmation before final assembly.
- Verify facts against original pages and quotations against source text, with four fact states, retained conflicts, material evidence links and adverse-use risks.
- Verify legal text, version, effectiveness and temporal application against accessed official sources. Apply civil or criminal elements, proof standards and rights to the actual role.
- Independently recompute material amounts, dates, ratios, subtotals and totals from source inputs and formulas. Record discrepancies and actual coverage.
- Check overstatement, admissions, adverse-use wording, necessary alternative defences and mandatory disclosure. Keep internal risk/settlement calculations separate from confirmed external positions.
- Check evidence supports the entire proposition. Allow all valuable evidence, core proof first, without a numerical cap.
- Check the conclusions table, pleading, requested catalogue, actual evidence materials and hearing/settlement positions agree. For each revision verify propagation to every affected item, with honest pending status for unfinished updates.
- Check effective versions, actual file existence and readability, delivery scope and confirmation history. Internal ledgers remain mandatory even if no files are requested.

Classify findings separately as factual error, legal error, judgment disagreement or insufficient evidence. Record impact and reuse any applicable existing severity scheme. A supported difference of judgment is not automatically an error. Missing evidence requires a calibrated conclusion. Unverified items and unperformed checks cannot be called verified. Material unresolved conflicts, stale linked outputs or missing section approvals hold final assembly/release as applicable. Retain verified conclusions with their review evidence alongside findings. This review remains part of the existing quality gate and release/sign-off controls.


For litigation artifact scope apply R8 over legacy same-run catalogue wording. Record actual procedural catalogue duties, resolver return codes, template approval/path/hash evidence and per-artifact fidelity checks. SELECTED alone does not establish approval of a candidate template. Verify privacy isolation extends to ledgers, strategy, work records and attachments before any repository/export operation. Keep capability evidence separate for analysis, text working drafts and formal-file generation, and mark unexecuted cases unverified.
